KEY INSIGHTS:
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Methods 1-4 (NetworkX, Manual, Matrix, Sparse) produce nearly identical
    results because they all implement the same mathematical PageRank formula.

  • Monte Carlo (Method 5) is stochastic - results vary with different random seeds
    and number of surfers. Increasing surfers improves accuracy.

  • HITS (Method 6) computes AUTHORITY scores, not PageRank. These are conceptually
    different: HITS measures 'quality of incoming links from good hubs' while
    PageRank measures 'probability a random surfer lands on the page'.

  • For this 5-node graph, the PageRank order is: C > A > D > B > E
    Page C is the hub (receives links from A, B, D)
    Page E is the dangling leaf (only receives, no outgoing)
